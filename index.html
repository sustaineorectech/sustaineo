The Critical Materials Challenge

A Crisis Hidden in Plain Sight

The clean energy transition is generating a new wave of complex waste streams laden with critical minerals - and conventional recycling is failing to recover them.

🖥️
60M+

Tonnes of E-Waste Annually

End-of-life PCBs and electronic components contain gold, silver, copper, platinum-group metals and rare earth elements - most lost to landfill or energy-intensive smelting with minimal recovery.

☀️
78M

Tonnes of Solar Waste by 2050

First-generation PV panels are entering end-of-life. Silicon, silver, aluminium, copper and EVA polymers are chemically bound together - current methods destroy value or create secondary contamination.

🌬️
50M+

Wind Blade Composite Waste

Decommissioned turbine blades contain rare earth magnets, glass fibre composites and structural polymers. No scalable circular solution exists - most end up in landfill or are incinerated.

Conventional recycling loses value, creates secondary waste, and relies on pyrometallurgical and acid-leach routes that are energy-intensive, hazardous, and incompatible with circular economy frameworks. A fundamentally better approach is required.
